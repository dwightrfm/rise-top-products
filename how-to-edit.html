Editing: Top Products

Change the slides right on the website. Phone, tablet, or computer. No downloads, no code.

Presenting link (send this to anyone)dwightrfm.github.io/rise-top-products/ Edit linkdwightrfm.github.io/rise-top-products/?edit

1Open the edit link

A bar shows at the top that says Editing. Use ‹ › to move between slides, or All slides to see them all.

2Change what you want

  • On a computer: click any words and type.
  • On a phone or tablet: tap any words. A box opens with every line on that slide. Change it, press Done.
  • Gold accent word: put *stars* around it in the box.
  • Pictures: gold dashed border. Tap one to upload a new one. QR codes can be remade from any link.

3Use it

  • Present: hides the bar and shows your version full size.
  • Saved on your device: your edits stay put. Open the edit link again on the same device and they are still there.
  • My copy: downloads your version as one file. Open it on any computer to present, even offline.

4Change it for the whole team (key holders only)

Publish to team changes the presenting link for everyone. It asks for the team edit key once per device. The live link updates in 1 to 2 minutes.

Nothing is ever lost. Every published version is kept and can be rolled back.
